Instruction - Exact Transcription

Exact Transcription

Welcome to the Real Effort Task - Transcription

Exact Transcription is an experimental task designed to measure participants' real effort through an accurate text transcription activity. In this task, participants are shown a text or sequence of characters and are required to type it exactly as displayed. The performance can be evaluated based on the amount and accuracy of the transcription they complete within a given time, also this task will have a limited amount of time to complete as many transcription tasks as possible.

How to Complete the Task

1. Read the text

A text will be displayed on the screen. Read the text carefully.

2. Transcribe the text

Type the text exactly as it appears. Capitalization, punctuation, spaces, and other characters must match the original text.

3. Submit your answer

Click the Submit Answer button when you have finished typing the text.

4. Continue

After submitting an answer, a new transcription task will appear automatically.

Important:

You will have 120 seconds to complete as many tasks as possible.
